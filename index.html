Research experience

2023 – 2024 · Undergraduate thesis, RUET

Delta/Fork Transformer Based 30-Pulse Star Rectifier for Input Current Harmonic Reduction

  • Designed and validated a delta/fork transformer-based 30-pulse star rectifier in MATLAB/Simulink.
  • Two parallel 15-pulse uncontrolled star rectifiers feed the load through an inter-phase transformer (IPT).
  • Input-current THD stayed below 5 % across load conditions, meeting IEEE 519, with stronger harmonic suppression than 6-, 12- and 18-pulse topologies.
  • Evaluated power quality and efficiency for high-current, low-voltage industrial loads.
